ReleasePilot Data Deletion Instructions

ReleasePilot is a local desktop app. Most project data is stored on the user's own computer.

To delete local ReleasePilot data, remove the app folder and any generated media folders created by the app. Stored API credentials can be removed from the local backend environment file.

To remove Facebook or Instagram access, open your Meta account settings and remove ReleasePilot/post 3 from connected business integrations or app connections. Published posts can be deleted directly inside Facebook, Instagram, or the relevant connected platform.
