ReleasePilot Privacy Policy

ReleasePilot is a local desktop tool for artists to prepare, review, schedule, and publish their own music content to connected social and video platforms.

Information We Process

ReleasePilot may process artist-provided catalogue metadata, local audio files, artwork, generated video files, publishing schedules, approval history, and account connection settings needed to publish approved content.

Meta and Social Platform Data

When a user connects Meta, ReleasePilot uses the permissions granted by the user to identify the connected Facebook Page and Instagram professional account, create media publishing containers, publish approved posts, and check publishing status.

Access tokens and API credentials are stored locally on the user's own machine in the app backend environment file. ReleasePilot does not sell, rent, or share these credentials.

Local Storage

ReleasePilot stores project settings, schedules, local review queues, and publishing history on the user's device. Files selected by the user remain under the user's control.

Third-Party Services

The app can connect to services such as Meta, YouTube, Cloudflare R2, Pexels, Pixabay, and other user-configured platforms. Each service may process data according to its own privacy policy and terms.

Data Deletion

Users can remove local schedules, history, generated files, and stored credentials from their machine. Platform posts or uploaded media can be deleted through the relevant connected platform account.
